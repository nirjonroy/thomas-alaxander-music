CROONERS

A celebration of timeless vocal artistry, where classic jazz, swing, and beloved standards are carried forward through Thomas Alexander's heritage-rooted voice. Shaped by decades of performance and lived musical experience, the show offers audiences the elegance, warmth, and emotional truth of the great crooner tradition while bringing Thomas's own distinctive voice to every phrase.

Thomas Alexander's artistry is shaped by his Black Indigenous, Copper Skin Creek, and Cherokee lineage, a heritage carried forward through generations and expressed through the emotional resonance of his voice. His interpretations of classic crooner and Platters repertoire honour memory, culture, and the living archive he represents as The Voice. As a cultural steward, Thomas brings forward a vocal tradition shaped by resilience, survival, and generational artistry.

Crooners invites audiences into a world of elegance, memory, and emotional depth. Every note is intentional. Every phrase is lived. The result is more than a collection of timeless songs: it is an intimate musical journey shaped by heritage, storytelling, and a lifetime devoted to the art of song.

Technical Performance Format

Duo - Vocalist + Keys
 A classic crooner configuration ideal for intimate rooms and soft-seat theatres. Clean, elegant, presenter-friendly.

Trio - Vocalist + Keys + Bass
 Adds warmth, depth, and rhythmic foundation. Perfect for jazz rooms, heritage venues, and small theatres.

Quartet - Vocalist + Keys + Bass + Drums
 A full small-ensemble experience with cinematic swing, jazz articulation, and classic crooner energy.

Full Ensemble - Vocalist + Keys + Bass + Drums + Guitar / Horns / Background Vocals
 A theatre-level production offering rich harmonic texture, expanded arrangements, and the full breadth of the Crooners repertoire. Ideal for festivals, large theatres, and gala events.

Short Bio

Thomas Alexander - The Voice - is a heritage vocalist, theatre-level artist, and storyteller whose career crosses jazz, soul, R&B, gospel, country, pop, and musical theatre. His artistry is grounded in decades of performance and in his Black Indigenous lineage, carrying Creek, Cherokee, and Yamassee roots forward through music.

A Rare Classic Vocal Interpreter, Thomas brings warmth, authority, storytelling, and lived musical experience to every performance. Rather than imitate the legendary singers who shaped this repertoire, he honours their artistry while bringing his own distinctive voice to the song.
