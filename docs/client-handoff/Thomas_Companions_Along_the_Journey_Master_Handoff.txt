SECTION 04: WHAT THE COMPANIONS ASK OF US
THE HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

The teaching now turns toward the visitor.

Heal the wounded.

Protect the innocent.

Remember what caused harm.

Carry forward what has been learned.

Respect what we have been permitted to witness.

Do not destroy what we have merely been privileged to behold.

Do not assume that everything beautiful must belong to us.

Prepare a path for those who follow, even when we ourselves may never walk that path.

Then:

Once we recognize the lesson, we become responsible for what we do with what we have learned.

SECTION 05: THE FINAL REST
CONTINUANCE

Rest itself has purpose.

Eventually there comes another kind of rest.

The final rest.

The seed continues.

Water continues flowing.

Wind continues carrying.

Sunlight reaches another generation.

Night returns.

Morning returns.

Another generation awakens.

The purpose continues.

Then:

What may appear to us as an ending is not.
 It is just a new beginning.
